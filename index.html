📖 Business User Metric Glossary

Gross Written Premium (GWP)
Total premium booked on booked reinsurance treaties converted to USD at valuation date rates.
Earned Premium
The portion of GWP earned over elapsed policy risk coverage periods.
Loss Ratio
Incurred Losses divided by Earned Premium. Measures primary underwriting profitability.
Combined Ratio
Loss Ratio + Commission Ratio + 5% Admin Expense. Values above 100% represent underwriting losses.
